About the Artist

The Somatic & Intuitive Process

My practice completely bypasses rigid, concept-driven planning. I do not begin a canvas with a strict rendering or a final image in mind. Instead, my work centers on a continuous flow of intuitive mark-making—a literal cycle of doodling, applying rich paint to canvas, and emoting.

Working primarily on large-scale acrylic canvases allows me the physical space to explore the translation of internal landscapes, weight, and presence into expressive figures. Every brushstroke is a direct response to the immediate physical moment, transforming abstract emotion into tangible form.

Based out of Raleigh, North Carolina, I develop my original collections and interactive digital pieces in my dedicated studio environment, focusing heavily on raw movement, texture, and deep exploration of form.
