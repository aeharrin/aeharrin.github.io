Studio Policies

Shipping & Delivery

Large canvases ship un-stretched and rolled carefully in heavy-duty tubes to ensure absolute protection. Local pickup or custom delivery is happily arranged for collectors within the Raleigh-Durham triangle area.

Commissions & Custom Work

My creative process is purely somatic and intuitive rather than concept-driven. Because of this, I do not accept rigid, illustrative commissions. However, custom color-palette scaling or large-scale landscape configurations can be discussed depending on my current studio schedule.
