Artist statement

My paintings are built up in layers — allowing the initial layers to be intuitive without worrying about the outcome. It is in these layers that my subconscious finds its voice, conveying emotions that I interpret and direct, shaping the overall feeling of the painting.
Artist photo

Ashley
Harrington

Ashley Harrington is a self-taught painter from Raleigh, North Carolina. Her figurative and abstract work explores self-awareness through emotional observance — painting as a practice of reconnection, identity, and inner excavation.

Her work has been exhibited at Artspace, Duke University Hospitals, Cameron Art Museum, and Industrious offices across the Triangle. She is a 2023 United Arts Council grant recipient.
